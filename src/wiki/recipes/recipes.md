## lemon bars

### crust
- 1 cup flower
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/4 tesp salt
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1 tesp vanilla extract
- light lemon zest

cook 375F for ~20 min, until lightly browned

### filling
- 1 cup sugar
- 1/4 cup flower
- 5 eggs
- 3/4 cup lemon juice
- heavy lemon zest

cook 350F for ~20 min, until filling set (no jiggling)

let sit for 2 hours to cool
sprinkle powdered sugar on top
